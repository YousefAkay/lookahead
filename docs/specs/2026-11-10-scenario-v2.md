# Scenario calculator v2, spec (2026-11-10)

## What it is
The "what if" calculator shows my chequing and savings together by default, so money I move between my own accounts no longer makes the year-end balance look far below zero. Family transfers count as income, a big lump sum is not averaged in, and the basis of each estimate is always visible under its field. It is still a calculator, not a forecast.

## Decisions
- **Combined view by default:** the starting balance is my latest chequing balance plus a savings balance I type in (savings is not imported). Transfers between my own accounts cancel out: they are neither income nor spending. (My chequing is refilled from savings, so chequing alone runs far below zero.)
- **No savings balance entered:** the page says clearly that it shows chequing only. (So I never mistake one view for the other.)
- **The savings balance is remembered** on this computer, with the day I entered it shown next to it; clearing it returns to chequing only. (I retype it only when it changes, and I can see when it's stale.)
- **Top-ups from savings:** the field stays for the chequing-only view, default $0, and is hidden when a savings balance is entered. (Counting it in the combined view would double count.)
- **Income:** family transfers count as income. Smaller, regular ones go into "income per month". A big one-off lump sum, by the shared one-time money rule (the forecaster's rule), is not averaged in. One that already arrived is part of today's balance; an expected future one is entered as a dated one-off item ("money in", for example a family transfer in January). (One rule everywhere, already tested.)
- **Basis notes always visible** under Income and Daily spend, including when nothing could be estimated: then the note says why (for example how many deposits there were and how many were left out as one-offs). (At my check on 2026-11-10 I could not see them; on the synthetic demo they show, but the income note is blank when no income is estimated and nothing was left out.)
- **The buffer and the "first day below the buffer" marker apply to whatever balance the chart shows:** the combined total in the default view, chequing in the chequing-only view. (The warning has to be about the line I'm looking at.)
- **Default end date:** 90 days after my last data date, still editable. (A year-end date grows less useful as the year goes on.)
- **2026-11-10, during the build: clearing is a "Clear (chequing only)" button** under the savings field (shown once a balance is entered), as well as emptying the field. (An explicit control is clearer, and the page tests can use it.)
- **2026-11-10, after the review: the daily spend counts everything not counted elsewhere.** Left out only rent, confirmed recurring series and transfers between my own accounts (and refunded pairs, which net to zero); card payments, e-Transfers to other people and withdrawals count. The spend note says so. (The old rule left out every transfer-typed row and understated my spending.)
- **2026-11-10, after the review: a warning in the chequing-only view** when a confirmed top-up from savings is listed and the top-ups field is not zero. (The same money could be counted twice.)
- **2026-11-17, the two open points (my decisions):** an ended rent series is dropped when another rent series is still active; an ended confirmed pay or income series is left out of income, same as spending.
- **2026-11-17, after my third real-data check:** big one-off payments (the shared one-time money rule, for money out) are also left out of the daily spend and counted in its note, and can be added back as a dated one-off money out; the one-off fields are a form sent with Add; an item outside the window says it is not counted; both notes show their counts one per labelled line.
- **2026-11-17, after my fourth real-data check:** the fields refill when the data source, account or last day of data changes (one-off items are kept when only the last day changes); the spend note gives the average without the 10 biggest payments counted (shown only with more than 10); the counts-only check shows where the income comes from.
- **Where it lives:** the same Scenario page; the starting point (chequing, savings, together) sits at the top of the "Your scenario" card.

## Out of scope
- Money moved from chequing to savings in the chequing-only view (it stays left out of spending, as now; the combined view handles it).
- Importing savings, savings interest, or any savings activity besides the balance I type.
- Any change to the forecaster or its one-time money rule.
- Navigation changes (the `nav-rework` branch is updated after this merges).

## Definition of done
1. With a savings balance entered, the starting balance shown is chequing plus savings, and both lines ("as things are now", "my scenario") start there.
2. On made-up data with transfers to and from savings, income per month and the daily spend are the same as on the same data without those transfers.
3. With no savings balance, a note says the view is chequing only and the top-ups field is shown; with a savings balance, the top-ups field is hidden.
4. The savings balance and the day it was entered survive a page reload; clearing it returns to chequing only.
5. A future lump sum entered as a dated one-off "money in" raises "my scenario" by exactly that amount from that day on, and not before.
6. On made-up data, regular incoming e-Transfers are counted in income per month and a big one-off deposit (by the shared rule) is left out, and the income note says how many were left out.
7. The Income and Daily spend notes are visible in every case, including when income cannot be estimated.
8. The end date defaults to 90 days after the last data date and can be changed.
9. The buffer line and the "first day below the buffer" marker are measured on the balance the chart shows: with a savings balance entered, the first day below is when the combined total drops below the buffer (not when chequing alone does); in the chequing-only view, when chequing does.
10. Tests on made-up data cover 1 to 9; the full suite passes; the reviewer passes the branch against this spec.

## My real-data checks
1. With no savings balance entered: the chequing-only note shows and the top-ups field is there (yes/no).
2. After entering my savings balance: the starting balance is chequing plus savings (yes/no), the top-ups field is hidden (yes/no), and the end balance looks plausible rather than far below zero (yes/no).
3. The Income and Daily spend notes are visible under their fields (yes/no), with their counts: deposits counted, one-off deposits left out, payments counted.
4. After reloading the page, the savings balance and its date are still there (yes/no).
5. An expected family transfer entered as a dated one-off "money in" steps "my scenario" up on that day (yes/no).
6. The end date shows 90 days after my last data date (yes/no).
