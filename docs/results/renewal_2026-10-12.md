# The renewal draw against the default (synthetic, development personas)

Made with `python -m eval.arms --arms 5 6 7 8 --report`. Method and prediction in DECISIONS.md (2026-10-12, the renewal draw built behind a switch). 20 development personas per set (the 12-month and the 24-month set are never pooled), 40 weekly origins, 500 futures, seed 42, weekday method, raw chances. Arm 5 is the default as merged, arm 6 the renewal draw (6 past gaps, 2 longer than the wait), arms 7 and 8 the same with a minimum of 4 and 8 gaps. Forecast metrics on origins not already in shortfall. Recall is given for both definitions: opened ahead / on ahead.
